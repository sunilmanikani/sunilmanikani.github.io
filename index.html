About

I'm a doctoral researcher focused on Generative AI and its applications to complex, real-world problems. My work spans diffusion-based generative models, deep learning architectures, and the application of AI to scientific domains like geophysics and climate science.

I'm particularly interested in how generative models can accelerate scientific discovery — from diffusion-based seismic monitoring for carbon capture and sequestration to building more efficient ML pipelines in systems like TensorFlow.

When I'm not training models, I'm thinking about how AI can be applied responsibly to solve meaningful problems at scale.
