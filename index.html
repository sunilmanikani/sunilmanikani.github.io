01 About

I'm a strategic AI leader and Senior Data Scientist at SLB (Schlumberger), where I've spent 13+ years driving end-to-end AI innovation — from seismic geo-body detection and energy transition modelling to production-grade agentic RAG systems and carbon trading infrastructure.

I specialize in taking Generative AI and advanced ML from research to real-world deployment. My work spans diffusion models, large reasoning models (fine-tuned Mistral & Qwen), agentic workflows, and high-performance ML systems — applied to some of the most complex problems in energy, geophysics, and sustainability.

Core member of SLB's Responsible AI steering committee. 10+ patents. Doctoral researcher in Generative AI at Golden Gate University (expected 2027). Peer reviewer for Geophysical Prospecting and contributor to the McFarland Academic Journal.
