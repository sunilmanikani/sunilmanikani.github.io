About

I'm a strategic AI leader and G14 Senior Data Scientist at SLB (Schlumberger), where I've spent 13+ years driving end-to-end AI innovation — from seismic geo-body detection and energy transition modelling to production-grade agentic RAG systems and carbon trading infrastructure.

I specialize in taking generative AI and advanced ML from research to real-world deployment. My work spans diffusion models, large reasoning models (fine-tuned Mistral & Qwen), agentic workflows, and high-performance ML systems — all applied to some of the most complex problems in energy, geophysics, and sustainability.

I serve on SLB's Responsible AI steering committee, hold 10+ patents, and am currently pursuing doctoral research in Generative AI at Golden Gate University (expected 2027).
